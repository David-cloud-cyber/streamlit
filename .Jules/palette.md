## 2026-07-16 - [Required Field Indicators & A11y]
**Learning:** Adding a 'required' prop to base form components (like Field) ensures visual consistency and accessibility (via aria-hidden asterisks) across the app. Status icons need both 'title' for tooltips and 'aria-label' for screen readers.
**Action:** Use the 'required' prop on Field components and ensure decorative icons are wrapped with aria-hidden="true".
