## 2025-05-22 - [Form accessibility: Required indicator alignment]
**Learning:** Using 'justify-content: space-between' on form labels can push the required asterisk far from the label text on wide screens, making it less discoverable and potentially confusing. 'flex-start' with a small 'gap' is safer for inline decorations.
**Action:** Always verify label alignment when adding decorations like required indicators or tooltips.
